    Example 10. Growing Dots.

    A pattern of growing dots is shown below.
    🔗
    
    An image showing a series of of 5 steps. Each step is a vertical rectangle containing circles. The number of circles increases with each step creating a larger isosceles right triangle after each step.
    🔗

    (a)

    Describe the pattern presented in the image. In particular, describe how each arrangement of dots is related to the arrangement in the step before it.
    🔗
    🔗

    (b)

    Determine the number of dots in each step of the pattern. Describe the numeric pattern.
    🔗
    🔗

    (c)

    If this pattern would continue, how many dots would be in step 10 of the pattern? How did you determine your answer?
