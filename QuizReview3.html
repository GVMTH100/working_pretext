    3.

    Generalize the solution to this polygon puzzle by writing the upper left corner as \(p\) and then writing the remaining vertices in terms of \(p\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    To find the upper right vertex we subtract \(p\) from 10. To find the lower left vertex we subtract \(p\) from 11. To find the lower right vertex we can subtract \(10-p\) from 13.
    \begin{equation*} 13-(10-p) \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} 13-10-(-p) \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} 3+p \end{equation*}
    To find the lower right vertex we could also have subtracted \(11-p\) from 14.
    \begin{equation*} 14-(11-p) \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} 14-11-(-p) \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} 3+p \end{equation*}
    🔗
