    Example 13. Plotting with technology.

    Open your browser to Desmos.com and recreate the graph from Example 12. Instructions for plotting in Desmos:
    🔗
    1. Plotting points with Desmos
      🔗
      1. Click on the add item menu, "+" (upper left). Choose table.
        🔗
      2. Enter the date from your table into Desmos.
        🔗
      3. Notice that points are being plotted as you type them.
        🔗
      4. You might not be able to see all of your points on the graph.
        🔗
      🔗
    2. Setting the graphing window with Desmos
      🔗
      1. Click on the wrench icon in the upper right corner.
        🔗
      2. For the independent variable, \(x\) enter the low and high values for your data.
        🔗
      3. Choose a reasonable step size. The step size is the interval between tick marks on the graph.
        🔗
      4. Include a title for the \(x\)-axis.
        🔗
      5. Repeat for the dependent variable, \(y\text{.}\)
        🔗
      🔗
    3. Graphing an equation
      🔗
      1. On the left side of the screen, type in the equation you want to plot in the next entry line.
        🔗
      2. It will automatically appear on the graphing screen.
        🔗
      3. Troubleshoot and change the equation if it doesn’t fit the data.
        🔗
      🔗
    🔗
