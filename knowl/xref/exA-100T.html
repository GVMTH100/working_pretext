(a)

Choose any number on the interior of the table. Describe in words what happens to the values as you move away from this number. Consider movement in multiple directions, up, down, left, right, and diagonally.
🔗
🔗

(b)

Consider a second number from the table. Do the same patterns apply to this number as the one you chose in part (a)?

(c)

Because our patterns remain consistent for any number chosen, we can generalize the rules in terms of a variable. Suppose that we choose one square from the interior of the table and replace the value with \(n\text{.}\) Write each of the values around this number in terms of \(n\text{,}\) using the patterns you described above.
