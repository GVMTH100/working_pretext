    Worksheet Mathematical Mindset 1
    

    In today’s reading you were introduced to the idea of a fixed mindset (performance-goal-orientation) compared to a growth mindset (learning-goal-orientation). As with most things we do not necessarily have a completely fixed or a completely growth mindset, instead we are probably on a continuum between the two. For example, we might believe we can learn new things and take on challenges to do so, but then falter when faced with criticism. We also vary in mindset based on the setting, for example, we might have a more growth mindset when it comes to learning to draw, but find ourselves in a more fixed mindset when it comes to learning a new language.
    🔗

    1.

    Think about your mindset in terms of learning mathematics. Where do you currently fall on the spectrum with the fixed mindset on the left and the growth mindset on the right.
    🔗

    (a)

    Beliefs about Human Potential
    🔗
    Everyone is born with certain skills and aptitudes. \(\bigcirc\) \(\bigcirc\) \(\bigcirc\) \(\bigcirc\) \(\bigcirc\) With effective strategies and time, people can improve their skills and aptitudes.
    🔗

    (b)

    Effort and Difficulty
    🔗
    Believe abilities and knowledge the come easily indicate natural talent, and that if something isn’t easy, it cannot be learned. \(\bigcirc\) \(\bigcirc\) \(\bigcirc\) \(\bigcirc\) \(\bigcirc\) Value the effort itself as a key element of gaining knowledge and mastery.
    🔗

    (c)

    Challenges and Obstacles
    🔗
    Generally avoid challenges and see obstacles as signs they are in the wrong direction. \(\bigcirc\) \(\bigcirc\) \(\bigcirc\) \(\bigcirc\) \(\bigcirc\) Seeks out new challenges and see obstacles as problems to solve and ways to grow.
    🔗

    (d)

    Mistakes and Failure
    🔗
    Hides or makes excuses for mistakes; becomes discouraged and frustrated by failure. \(\bigcirc\) \(\bigcirc\) \(\bigcirc\) \(\bigcirc\) \(\bigcirc\) Takes ownership of mistakes and understands that failure often leads to learning and long-term success.
    🔗

    (e)

    Feedback, Criticism, and Suggestions
    🔗
    Rejects negative feedback and can become defensive; likely focuses on positive feedback. \(\bigcirc\) \(\bigcirc\) \(\bigcirc\) \(\bigcirc\) \(\bigcirc\) Appreciates the perspectives of others and welcomes candid feedback.
    🔗

    (f)

    Outlook on the Future
    🔗
    Success is limited to a few specific areas along a firmly defined pathway. \(\bigcirc\) \(\bigcirc\) \(\bigcirc\) \(\bigcirc\) \(\bigcirc\) Success is possible in many areas, once individuals create their own pathways.
    🔗
    🔗

    2.

    Choose one of the areas in question 1 and discuss where you are currently in terms of mindset and how you might transition to or maintain a growth mindset.
